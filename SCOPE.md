# Product Scope and Boundaries

**Authority:** signed BRD/SRS v1.4. This page summarizes rather than replaces the signed documents.

## In scope
1. Login and role/field-level access control.
2. Academic-year selection and maintenance.
3. Admission form, student/parent details, required documents, missing-document checklist, drafts, submission, Principal review/decision, status history and seat capacity.
4. Receipt types, fee heads, annual fee structures, vehicle places and Principal approval.
5. Student fee assignment; variable Admission Fee; offline payments by receipt type/fee head; balances, payment history and outstanding list.
6. Principal-approved concession, extra charge, refund and correction flows using auditable entries.
7. Numbered receipt per payment; final fees receipt only after full payment; unnumbered statement available any time; duplicate/reprint audit; approved PDF layouts.
8. Student search/profile/history, explicit Left/Transferred action and permanent record history.
9. Annual LC/TC records, two-step review/approval, generation from stored data, retrieval/reprint.
10. Audit log, basic dashboard, synthetic demo data, tests, technical notes, setup guide and source handover.

## Out of scope
- Online payment collection/gateway or software holding/processing money.
- Fixed first payment/minimum 10%, fixed installment schedule, due-date plan, automatic fee reminders.
- Parent/student portal, SMS/WhatsApp/email notifications, native mobile app.
- Promotion, attendance, exams, timetable or unrelated academic modules.
- Automatic fee/balance carry-forward and year-end unpaid-fee closure; school handles year end outside the system.
- Languages other than English; real student-data import.
- Production deployment, installation, hosting/domain, HTTPS/live security, database operation, backups/restore/monitoring, day-to-day operation, training and maintenance (Client responsibilities unless a signed change request says otherwise).

## Acceptance boundaries
- Confirmed mock-up layouts do not mean high-resolution letterhead and complete print assets have been delivered.
- “And many more” receipt types is not permission to invent forms; Client must confirm the catalog/configuration policy.
- Technical suggestions in this pack do not change signed scope.
- Open behaviors (seat-cap unit, exact receipt catalog, books/uniform policy, TC-with-dues policy, etc.) need written resolution or a documented approved interim rule.
