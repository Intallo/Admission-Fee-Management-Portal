## 3. Residual Operational Risks and Client Risk Acceptance

The following unavoidable operational risks belong to live client hosting and school business operations. They are formally documented for client sign-off:

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Potential Impact | Recommended Client Mitigation | Designated Risk Owner |
|---|---|---|---|:---:|
| **RSK-01** | **Physical Ink Signature Requirement on Receipts** | Receipts are legally valid only when physically ink-signed by Payer, Cashier, and Manager (Principal) per signed requirements (DEC-05). Unsigned printouts may cause audit disputes. | Enforce school administrative policy requiring Cashier and Principal to sign physical printouts at the fee collection counter before handing to parent. | **Client Principal** (Rakesh Aralikatti) |
| **RSK-02** | **Disaster Recovery & Backup Automation** | Vendor delivers software; live database server, nightly backups, and offsite storage are client-operated. Hardware loss without backups would result in data loss. | Execute the automated nightly `pg_dump` cron script detailed in [docs/BACKUP_AND_RESTORE_GUIDE.md](file:///docs/BACKUP_AND_RESTORE_GUIDE.md). Test restoration quarterly. | **Client System Admin** |
| **RSK-03** | **Network Boundary & Production TLS Hardening** | Exposing PostgreSQL port 5432 directly to the public internet or running over plain HTTP would expose traffic to interception. | Deploy behind Nginx reverse proxy with TLS 1.3 encryption; bind PostgreSQL port 5432 strictly to localhost or private VPC subnet. | **Client Infrastructure Team** |
| **RSK-04** | **Default Demo Credentials Replacement** | Seed accounts have standard initial passwords (`School@2026!`). Failing to change default credentials in production would allow unauthorized staff access. | System Administrator must change initial passwords for all staff members immediately upon production deployment before user onboarding. | **Client System Admin** |
| **RSK-05** | **Offline Payment Verification** | The portal records offline payments (cash, cheques, UPI manual); it does not integrate with banking APIs. Fake or bounced cheques recorded as cash could create accounting discrepancies. | School accounts office must verify physical bank deposit receipts before issuing Final Fees Receipts. Reversal workflow (US-26A) is available if a cheque bounces. | **Client Accounts Officer** |
